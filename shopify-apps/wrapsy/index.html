Wrapsy: Gift Wrap & Message

Offer gift wrap and gift messages in the cart and on products.

View on the Shopify App StoreSetup guide

Overview

What it does

Gift wrap and gift messages on product pages and in the cart, ready to print on packing slips.

Set up wrap options with their own prices. The app creates one hidden gift wrap product, so prices, taxes and reports work like any other product. Shoppers add wrap per item or for the whole order, with a message and To and From names, within limits you choose. Settings live in your Shopify store, so the storefront keeps working if our app is slow. Messages show on orders and packing slips. On Pro, a Shopify POS tile adds gift wrap and a message to in-store sales.

  • Gift wrap per item on the product page or for the whole order in the cart
  • Gift message with To/From names, character limit and required option
  • Wrap options are variants of a hidden product: normal prices and taxes
  • Storefront keeps working even if the app is offline
  • Messages on orders and packing slips, optional hide-prices note
