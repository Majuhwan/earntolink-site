Overview

What it does

Let shoppers add a donation or round up their order in the cart, paid at checkout, with real totals.

Kindcart adds a donation block to your cart or product page. Shoppers pick a fixed amount or tick an unchecked box to round up, and the donation is paid like any item through Shopify Checkout. One hidden donation product keeps its variants in sync with your amounts. Totals come from paid orders minus refunds. On Pro, a Shopify POS tile lets staff add a round-up or fixed donation to in-store sales. The app never holds money: donations are paid to you and you pass them on to your cause.

  • Donation buttons on cart or product page
  • Round-up box, never pre-checked
  • Paid through the normal checkout
  • Real totals from paid orders minus refunds
  • Goal bar with the real amount raised
