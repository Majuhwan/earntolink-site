Rowsmith: Bulk Product Editor

Bulk edit products and variants in a grid, and undo any job.

View on the Shopify App StoreSetup guide

Overview

What it does

Bulk edit products and variants in a spreadsheet grid. Review every change and undo any job.

Rowsmith opens your products and variants in a grid you can edit like a spreadsheet. Filter, pick the columns (title, vendor, type, tags, status, SEO, SKU, barcode, weight and more), paste blocks from Excel or Google Sheets and fill one value into many rows. Review every changed cell before saving. Rows edited by someone else meanwhile are skipped, not overwritten. Every job keeps the old values: undo in one click, or on Pro schedule the undo so a sale ends by itself.

  • Spreadsheet grid for products and variants
  • Paste blocks of cells from Excel or Sheets
  • Review every changed cell before saving
  • End a sale in one step and undo it on a date you pick
  • One-click undo of any job
