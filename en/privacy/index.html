1. General

EarnToLink (언투링크, "EarnToLink") protects personal information under the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea. This policy covers the website earntolink.com and the apps EarnToLink makes.

Summary: the website uses no sign-up, forms, cookies, analytics or ads. Our apps process files and photos only on the device and never send them anywhere.

2. Website

  • No sign-up, login or forms.
  • No cookies, analytics tools or ad scripts.
  • The website is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub may process access logs such as IP addresses for operation and security, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • The Pretendard font is loaded from the jsDelivr CDN, which may receive request information such as your browser's IP address.
  • If cookies or analytics are ever introduced, this policy will be updated first.

4. Our apps

Covered apps: Roll Tidy (정리 싹), Roll Flick (넘겨 정리), Space Map (용량 지도), Dupeless (중복 싹), Archive Fix (한글 압축). All are coming soon.

  • The apps do not connect to the network and never upload files or photos.
  • No accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking.
  • Deleted files and photos go to the system trash (Recently Deleted on iOS) and can be restored.
  • Settings, progress records (Roll Flick) and photo analysis caches (Roll Tidy: hash, sharpness, brightness) stay in app storage on the device.
  • Payments are handled by each app store. EarnToLink never receives payment details.

5. Client projects

When a client project involves the client's personal data, the scope and method of processing follow the contract and data processing agreement with that client.

6. Disclosure to third parties

We do not provide personal information to third parties, except where required by law.

7. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing at any time. Send requests to hello@earntolink.com; we act on them without delay.

8. Deletion

Personal information is destroyed without delay once its retention period ends or its purpose is achieved. Electronic files are deleted so they cannot be recovered.

9. Security

Access to inquiry email is limited to the representative, and we collect no more personal information than needed.
