How-to

Record the screen

  1. Choose full screen, a window, or drag a region, and pick system audio and microphone.
  2. Start recording. Press Ctrl+Shift+F9 to stop at any time. The free version records up to 5 minutes.

Edit zooms

  1. Zooms are added automatically where you clicked or typed. On the timeline, add, delete, move, or stretch them.
  2. Set the background, padding, rounded corners, and shadow, and trim the start and end.

Export a video

  1. Select Export, choose the aspect ratio and resolution, and save as MP4. The graphics card encoder is used when available.

Features

  • Automatic smooth zooms that follow your clicks and typing
  • Editable zoom timeline: level, timing, ease and focus point
  • Smoothed bigger cursor, click ripples and shortcut badges
  • Backgrounds, padding, rounded corners, shadow and webcam bubble (custom backgrounds and webcam: Pro)
  • Separate tracks for screen, computer sound, microphone and webcam (webcam: Pro)
  • Export MP4 up to 4K 60 fps or GIF, offline, one-time Pro (above 1080p 30 fps and GIF: Pro)

FAQ

What is Pro?

Pro is a one-time add-on purchase in the Microsoft Store, not a subscription. It works on Windows PCs signed in with the same Microsoft account.

How do I get Pro back on another PC or after reinstalling?

Sign in to the Microsoft Store with the account used for the purchase, then select Help > Restore purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Payments and refunds are handled by Microsoft. Go to account.microsoft.com > Order history, select the order, and request a refund. Microsoft’s refund policy applies.

Does the app need the internet? Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Files and input are processed only on this PC and are never uploaded. There are no accounts, ads, or tracking. The internet is used only for Microsoft Store purchases and updates.

Which file formats are supported?

Export: MP4 (1080p; 1440p, 4K, and 60 fps with Pro) and GIF (Pro). Background images: PNG, JPG.

How do I update the app?

The Microsoft Store installs updates automatically. To check now, open Microsoft Store > Library and select Get updates.

Troubleshooting

The recording has no sound.

Check that system audio and microphone were turned on before recording, and that the right devices are selected. Microphone access must be allowed in Windows privacy settings.

The preview stutters or graphics look wrong.

Update the graphics driver from your PC or GPU maker. On laptops, plug in the power adapter and use the Best performance power mode.

The app can’t save or export.

The folder may be read-only or the disk may be full. Choose another folder, such as Documents. If Controlled folder access is on in Windows Security, allow this app.

I bought Pro, but features are still locked.

Select Help > Restore purchase. Make sure the Microsoft Store is signed in with the account used for the purchase. Right after buying, confirmation can take a few minutes.

The app doesn’t start or stops responding.

Close and reopen the app. If it continues, go to Settings > Apps > Installed apps, open the app’s Advanced options, and select Repair. Reset also clears app settings.
