How-to

Cut silences

  1. Open a recording. Silences are found automatically.
  2. Choose a preset (Tight, Normal, Relaxed) or set the threshold, minimum silence, and padding. The time saved updates immediately.

Review and adjust cuts

  1. Play the preview; cut parts are skipped. Use J/K/L to play, C to cut or keep, and I/O/X to cut a range.
  2. Pro: also find filler words (um, uh) in 9 languages. Speech recognition runs on this PC.

Export

  1. Select Export and choose MP4, audio only, or SRT captions. The free version exports files up to 15 minutes.
  2. Pro: export cut lists for Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve (FCPXML), Premiere Pro (XML), or EDL, and batch-process several recordings.

Features

  • Finds silences instantly, filler words with on-device speech recognition (filler words: Pro)
  • Waveform timeline: keep, cut, resize or add cuts, undo and redo
  • Preview that skips the cuts, J/K/L and keyboard editing
  • Export MP4 (frame-accurate or smart cut), audio and SRT subtitles (free: files up to 15 minutes)
  • Cut lists for Final Cut, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro and EDL (Pro)
  • Batch processing, offline, no account, one-time Pro (batch: Pro)

FAQ

What is Pro?

Pro is a one-time add-on purchase in the Microsoft Store, not a subscription. It works on Windows PCs signed in with the same Microsoft account.

How do I get Pro back on another PC or after reinstalling?

Sign in to the Microsoft Store with the account used for the purchase, then select Help > Restore purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Payments and refunds are handled by Microsoft. Go to account.microsoft.com > Order history, select the order, and request a refund. Microsoft’s refund policy applies.

Does the app need the internet? Is my data sent anywhere?

Files and input are processed only on this PC and are never uploaded. Some features download a model or component once, the first time you use them; after that they work offline. There are no accounts, ads, or tracking.

Which file formats are supported?

Open: video and audio files that Windows can play, such as MP4, MOV, MKV, MP3, WAV, M4A. Export: MP4, M4A, MP3, WAV, FLAC, SRT; FCPXML, Premiere XML, and EDL with Pro.

How do I update the app?

The Microsoft Store installs updates automatically. To check now, open Microsoft Store > Library and select Get updates.

Troubleshooting

The preview shows no picture.

Preview uses Windows Media Foundation. Some codecs, such as HEVC, need the matching extension from the Microsoft Store. Export still works.

The preview stutters or graphics look wrong.

Update the graphics driver from your PC or GPU maker. On laptops, plug in the power adapter and use the Best performance power mode.

Processing is slow.

Large or many files take longer. Close other heavy apps, keep the laptop plugged in, and keep the PC from sleeping until the task finishes.

I bought Pro, but features are still locked.

Select Help > Restore purchase. Make sure the Microsoft Store is signed in with the account used for the purchase. Right after buying, confirmation can take a few minutes.

The app doesn’t start or stops responding.

Close and reopen the app. If it continues, go to Settings > Apps > Installed apps, open the app’s Advanced options, and select Repair. Reset also clears app settings.
