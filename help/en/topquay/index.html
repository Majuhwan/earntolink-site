How-to

Use the notch

  1. Select Show notch. A small bar appears at the top centre of the screen; clicks pass through it to the window below.
  2. Point at the bar to open it: media now playing with controls, timer and stopwatch, battery. Move away and it closes.
  3. Closing the settings window keeps the notch running. Open settings again from the notification-area icon.

Media controls

  1. Apps that support Windows media controls (music players, browsers, video apps) show the title, artist, artwork and progress.
  2. Use Previous, Play/Pause and Next in the open notch.

Size, position and colors

  1. Appearance changes the size and moves the notch left or right, for example to sit over a laptop camera.
  2. Color themes, the large size, another display, the clipboard and CPU/memory widgets and Pomodoro are part of Pro.

Features

  • Slim bar at the top of the screen, click-through while closed
  • Now playing with artwork and Previous / Play / Next
  • Timer with presets and an alert, stopwatch with laps
  • Battery level and time left, date and time
  • Hides during full-screen apps, adjustable position, per-monitor scaling
  • Color themes, clipboard and CPU/memory widgets, Pomodoro, other displays (Pro)

FAQ

What is Pro?

Pro is a one-time add-on purchase in the Microsoft Store, not a subscription. It works on Windows PCs signed in with the same Microsoft account.

How do I get Pro back on another PC or after reinstalling?

Sign in to the Microsoft Store with the account used for the purchase, then select Help > Restore purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Payments and refunds are handled by Microsoft. Go to account.microsoft.com > Order history, select the order, and request a refund. Microsoft’s refund policy applies.

Does the app need the internet? Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Files and input are processed only on this PC and are never uploaded. There are no accounts, ads, or tracking. The internet is used only for Microsoft Store purchases and updates.

How do I update the app?

The Microsoft Store installs updates automatically. To check now, open Microsoft Store > Library and select Get updates.

Troubleshooting

The notch disappears.

While a full-screen game, video or presentation runs on that display, the notch hides and comes back afterwards. Turn off Settings > Hide during full-screen apps to keep it. Exclusive full-screen games always cover it.

Media doesn’t appear.

Only apps that report to Windows media controls appear (the same ones as in the Windows volume flyout). Start playback in the app, then point at the notch.

I bought Pro, but features are still locked.

Select Help > Restore purchase. Make sure the Microsoft Store is signed in with the account used for the purchase. Right after buying, confirmation can take a few minutes.

How do I change the app language?

The app follows the Windows display language. Change it in Settings > Time & language > Language & region, then restart the app.

Contact support doesn’t open my email app.

If no default email app is set, select Copy info and send the text to hello@earntolink.com from any email service.

Contact support

Email hello@earntolink.com with the app name and version. In the app, Help > Contact support adds this information automatically.
