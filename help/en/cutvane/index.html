How-to

Edit on the timeline

  1. Drag clips from the media library onto the timeline. Gaps close automatically and linked clips move together.
  2. Use J/K/L to play, split at the playhead, and ripple delete. Effects, transitions, and titles preview without rendering.

Make a trailer

  1. Choose a trailer template, fill each shot slot and title card, and the music is matched for you.

Export the movie

  1. Select Export, choose resolution and quality, and save as MP4. The free version exports up to 1080p with no watermark.

Features

  • Real-time GPU preview: effects, transitions and titles play instantly
  • Multicam (up to 4 angles), compound clips, magnetic multi-track timeline
  • 30 filters, curves, scopes; color wheels, color match and LUTs (Pro)
  • 40 transitions and 40 animated titles (15 and 12 free)
  • Track mixer with EQ and compressor, 30 music tracks, 200 sound effects
  • Caption editor, SRT/WebVTT, chapter markers in MP4
  • Export 1080p with no watermark; 4K, HEVC, HDR, ProRes, AV1 (Pro)

FAQ

What is Pro?

Pro is a one-time add-on purchase in the Microsoft Store, not a subscription. It works on Windows PCs signed in with the same Microsoft account.

How do I get Pro back on another PC or after reinstalling?

Sign in to the Microsoft Store with the account used for the purchase, then select Help > Restore purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Payments and refunds are handled by Microsoft. Go to account.microsoft.com > Order history, select the order, and request a refund. Microsoft’s refund policy applies.

Does the app need the internet? Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Files and input are processed only on this PC and are never uploaded. There are no accounts, ads, or tracking. The internet is used only for Microsoft Store purchases and updates.

Which file formats are supported?

Import: MP4, MOV, MKV, and other video Windows can play; MP3, WAV, M4A audio; JPG and PNG images; OpenTimelineIO and EDL. Export: MP4 up to 1080p (4K, 1440p, and HEVC with Pro); OpenTimelineIO, EDL, FCPXML.

How do I update the app?

The Microsoft Store installs updates automatically. To check now, open Microsoft Store > Library and select Get updates.

Troubleshooting

Phone videos look washed out.

Phone HDR video (HLG or PQ) is tone-mapped automatically. If it still looks flat, apply a color look or adjust exposure and contrast on the clip.

The preview stutters or graphics look wrong.

Update the graphics driver from your PC or GPU maker. On laptops, plug in the power adapter and use the Best performance power mode.

Processing is slow.

Large or many files take longer. Close other heavy apps, keep the laptop plugged in, and keep the PC from sleeping until the task finishes.

I bought Pro, but features are still locked.

Select Help > Restore purchase. Make sure the Microsoft Store is signed in with the account used for the purchase. Right after buying, confirmation can take a few minutes.

The app doesn’t start or stops responding.

Close and reopen the app. If it continues, go to Settings > Apps > Installed apps, open the app’s Advanced options, and select Repair. Reset also clears app settings.
