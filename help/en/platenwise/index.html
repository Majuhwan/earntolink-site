How-to

Scan with a scanner

  1. Open Scanner and choose the device. Scanners with a WIA driver are listed; network scanners appear after Windows adds them in Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Printers & scanners.
  2. Choose Flatbed or Feeder (Both sides for duplex feeders), the resolution and the color mode, then select Scan. Repeat Scan to add more pages; 600 dpi and higher need Pro.
  3. Turn on Print after scan to use the scanner and printer as a copier.

Phone photos and camera

  1. Drag photos into the window or select Import. Camera takes a picture with a webcam.
  2. The page edges are found automatically and the page is straightened. In Crop, drag a corner to fix it; Auto finds the edges again, Full image keeps the whole photo.
  3. Choose Color, Grayscale, Black and white or Original. Color removes shadows and makes the paper white.

Save a PDF

  1. Arrange the pages (move up or down, rotate, delete), choose the format, page size and file size, then select Save. All pages go into one PDF.
  2. Pro: Searchable PDF adds an invisible text layer (Windows OCR) so you can search and copy text; Password protects the PDF; Remove blank pages and Split at blank pages help with feeder batches.

Copy text from a page

  1. Select a page and Copy text. Text is recognized on this PC with the OCR languages installed in Windows; choose the language in Settings or leave Automatic.

Import a folder (Pro)

  1. Open Folder import, choose a folder and select Add to document. Images are added in name order with page edges found automatically.

Features

  • Scan from WIA scanners: flatbed, feeder, both sides
  • Phone photos: page edges found and straightened automatically
  • Shadow-free color, grayscale and black and white pages
  • Multi-page PDF, JPG, PNG, TIFF and printing (free)
  • Searchable PDF with Windows OCR, blank-page removal (Pro)
  • Offline, no account, one-time Pro, no subscription

FAQ

What is Pro?

Pro is a one-time add-on purchase in the Microsoft Store, not a subscription. It works on Windows PCs signed in with the same Microsoft account.

How do I get Pro back on another PC or after reinstalling?

Sign in to the Microsoft Store with the account used for the purchase, then select Help > Restore purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Payments and refunds are handled by Microsoft. Go to account.microsoft.com > Order history, select the order, and request a refund. Microsoft’s refund policy applies.

Does the app need the internet? Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Files and input are processed only on this PC and are never uploaded. There are no accounts, ads, or tracking. The internet is used only for Microsoft Store purchases and updates.

How do I update the app?

The Microsoft Store installs updates automatically. To check now, open Microsoft Store > Library and select Get updates.

Troubleshooting

My scanner is not listed.

Platenwise uses WIA, the scanner interface built into Windows. Install the scanner's driver from its maker, check that Windows Scan or Windows Fax and Scan sees it, then select Refresh. Network scanners must be added in Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Printers & scanners first.

The page edges are wrong.

Edges are found best when the page lies on a darker surface with all four corners visible. In Crop, drag each corner onto the page corner, or choose Full image.

Text is not recognized, or in the wrong language.

Recognition uses the OCR languages installed in Windows. Add the language in Settings > Time & language > Language & region (with optical character recognition), then choose it under OCR language in Platenwise settings.

I bought Pro, but features are still locked.

Select Help > Restore purchase. Make sure the Microsoft Store is signed in with the account used for the purchase. Right after buying, confirmation can take a few minutes.

How do I change the app language?

The app follows the Windows display language. Change it in Settings > Time & language > Language & region, then restart the app.

Contact support doesn’t open my email app.

If no default email app is set, select Copy info and send the text to hello@earntolink.com from any email service.

The app doesn’t start or stops responding.

Close and reopen the app. If it continues, go to Settings > Apps > Installed apps, open the app’s Advanced options, and select Repair. Reset also clears app settings.

Contact support

Email hello@earntolink.com with the app name and version. In the app, Help > Contact support adds this information automatically.
