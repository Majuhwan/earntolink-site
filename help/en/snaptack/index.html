How-to

Take a screenshot

  1. Press the capture shortcut or select a mode: region, window, active window, or full screen.
  2. In region mode, click to snap to a window, panel, or button, or drag to select. The magnifier helps place the edges exactly.

Mark up a capture

  1. Use arrows, shapes, highlighter, text, numbered steps, blur, or spotlight. Undo and redo are unlimited.
  2. Copy, save, or drag the image into another app.

Copy text from the screen

  1. Select Copy text and drag over any area. Windows text recognition reads it offline; QR codes are read too. The free version allows 10 times a day.

Features

  • Region, window, full-screen and scrolling capture with magnifier and window / element snapping (scrolling: Pro)
  • Arrows, text, numbered steps, highlighter, blur and pixelate, spotlight and magnifier callouts (step and style presets: Pro)
  • Copy text from anything on screen with offline Windows OCR; QR codes are read too (free: 10 a day)
  • Searchable library: reopen captures with their markup, find them by text, title or tag
  • Save PNG, JPG, WEBP or PDF, copy as image or file, drag into any app
  • Global shortcuts, tray app, multi-monitor and high-DPI ready — no account, fully offline

FAQ

What is Pro?

Pro is a one-time add-on purchase in the Microsoft Store, not a subscription. It works on Windows PCs signed in with the same Microsoft account.

How do I get Pro back on another PC or after reinstalling?

Sign in to the Microsoft Store with the account used for the purchase, then select Help > Restore purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Payments and refunds are handled by Microsoft. Go to account.microsoft.com > Order history, select the order, and request a refund. Microsoft’s refund policy applies.

Does the app need the internet? Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Files and input are processed only on this PC and are never uploaded. There are no accounts, ads, or tracking. The internet is used only for Microsoft Store purchases and updates.

Which file formats are supported?

Save: PNG, JPG, WEBP, PDF. Import: images from files or the clipboard.

How do I update the app?

The Microsoft Store installs updates automatically. To check now, open Microsoft Store > Library and select Get updates.

Troubleshooting

The Print Screen key opens another tool.

In Windows Settings > Accessibility > Keyboard, turn off Use the Print screen key to open screen capture, then turn on the Print Screen option in this app.

Part of the capture is black.

Some apps, such as protected video players, block screen capture. Those areas can’t be captured.

I bought Pro, but features are still locked.

Select Help > Restore purchase. Make sure the Microsoft Store is signed in with the account used for the purchase. Right after buying, confirmation can take a few minutes.

How do I change the app language?

The app follows the Windows display language. Change it in Settings > Time & language > Language & region, then restart the app.

The app doesn’t start or stops responding.

Close and reopen the app. If it continues, go to Settings > Apps > Installed apps, open the app’s Advanced options, and select Repair. Reset also clears app settings.
