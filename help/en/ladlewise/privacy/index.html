Ladlewise privacy policy

Ladlewise does not collect, store, or transmit personal information.

Files you open or create and anything you type are processed only on your PC. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

Purchases and license checks are handled by the Microsoft Store under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Only when you select Download page: Ladlewise then downloads that one page and its recipe photo. Recipes, photos, meal plans and shopping lists stay on this PC and are never uploaded. No accounts, ads or tracking; Microsoft Store handles purchases and updates.

If you email support, your address and message are used only to reply.

Questions: hello@earntolink.com
