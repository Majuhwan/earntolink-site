How-to

Start a song

  1. Choose a genre template or a demo song, or start empty.
  2. Drag loops from the browser onto tracks; they always play in the song’s tempo and key.

Record audio or MIDI

  1. Arm a track, choose the input, and press Record. A count-in plays first; loop recording keeps every take.

Mix and export

  1. Set volume, pan, and sends in the mixer, and add effects to each track.
  2. Select Export and choose WAV, MP3, or MIDI.

Features

  • 47 sampled instruments from CC0 recordings, 12 synth engines and 1,008 loops in 8 genres
  • Drummer that plays along: 8 genres x 3 styles, complexity and loudness pad, fills, follows your bass
  • Live loops grid with scenes, song arrangement, piano roll, step sequencer and score view with printing
  • Recording with takes and comping, fades, time-stretch, beat alignment and pitch correction
  • 8 guitar and bass amps, 14 pedals, tuner, 28 effects, loudness meter, VST3 and CLAP plug-ins
  • Export WAV, MP3, M4A, FLAC, stems, MIDI and DAWproject; low-latency exclusive mode

FAQ

What is Pro?

Pro is a one-time add-on purchase in the Microsoft Store, not a subscription. It works on Windows PCs signed in with the same Microsoft account.

How do I get Pro back on another PC or after reinstalling?

Sign in to the Microsoft Store with the account used for the purchase, then select Help > Restore purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Payments and refunds are handled by Microsoft. Go to account.microsoft.com > Order history, select the order, and request a refund. Microsoft’s refund policy applies.

Does the app need the internet? Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Files and input are processed only on this PC and are never uploaded. There are no accounts, ads, or tracking. The internet is used only for Microsoft Store purchases and updates.

Which file formats are supported?

Import: WAV, MP3, FLAC, and other common audio; MIDI. Export: WAV, MP3, MIDI; FLAC, M4A, stems, and DAWproject with Pro.

How do I update the app?

The Microsoft Store installs updates automatically. To check now, open Microsoft Store > Library and select Get updates.

Troubleshooting

Recording is delayed or crackles.

In audio settings, choose your audio interface and adjust the buffer size: smaller for less delay, larger if it crackles. Close other audio apps.

There’s no sound.

Check the output device and volume in Windows sound settings and the volume mixer. If another app is using the audio device exclusively, close it.

The microphone doesn’t work.

In Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone, turn on Microphone access and Let desktop apps access your microphone. Then select the correct input device in the app.

I bought Pro, but features are still locked.

Select Help > Restore purchase. Make sure the Microsoft Store is signed in with the account used for the purchase. Right after buying, confirmation can take a few minutes.

The app doesn’t start or stops responding.

Close and reopen the app. If it continues, go to Settings > Apps > Installed apps, open the app’s Advanced options, and select Repair. Reset also clears app settings.
