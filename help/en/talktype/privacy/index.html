Talktype privacy policy

Talktype does not collect, store, or transmit personal information.

Files you open or create and anything you type are processed only on your PC. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

Files and input are processed only on this PC and are never uploaded. Some features download a model or component once, the first time you use them; after that they work offline. There are no accounts, ads, or tracking.

If you email support, your address and message are used only to reply.
