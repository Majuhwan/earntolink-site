Pipette theme documentation

Pipette is a theme for beauty and skin care stores that sell in steps: a cleanser, a treatment and a protector, in the right shade and size. This guide covers setup, every Pipette-specific feature, and common questions.

Version: 1.0.0. Support: see the support page.

1. Getting started

  1. In your Shopify admin, go to Online Store > Themes and add Pipette to your theme library.
  2. Click Customize to open the theme editor. Pipette installs with the same layout, colors and fonts as the demo store; demo images are not copied, so image spots show placeholders until you add your own.
  3. Before publishing, set up the items in sections 3 to 6 below: routine collections, shade swatches, samples and texture details.

Tip: duplicate your theme before editing code, and keep the original as a backup.

3. Routine steps (routine bar, routine builder, card labels)

Pipette organizes products into steps such as Cleanse, Treat and Protect.

  1. Create one collection per step (for example Cleanse, Treat, Protect).
  2. In the theme editor, open the Routine bar (overlay group, visible on every page) and choose a collection for each Step block. Give each step a label.
  3. Add the Routine builder section to your home page and choose the same collections for its steps.
  4. Product cards in those collections show a label such as "Step 2 · Treat" automatically.
  5. Customers pick one product per step; the routine bar stays docked at the bottom of the page and adds the whole routine to the cart in one click.

Routine offer text

The routine builder's Offer blocks show text such as "3 steps · Save 15%". The theme does not calculate discounts. To make the offer real, create an automatic discount in Discounts (for example: 15% off products in the Cleanse, Treat and Protect collections with a minimum quantity of 3). The cart and checkout then show the discount and the savings line. Keep the offer text and the discount in sync.

4. Shades and swatches

Color swatches appear in the variant picker, on product cards and in the shade drawer.

  1. In the product, set a Category that supports color (for example Foundations, Lipstick or Blush).
  2. Edit the variant option (for example Shade) and connect it to the category's color metafield, then pick a color or upload an image for each value.
  3. Options with many values open the shade drawer: set Swatches shown before the shade drawer (4-16) on the Variant picker block.
  4. The Shade finder section groups shades into families. In each Shade family block, list the start of each option value (for example F10, F20).

Without connected swatches, options show as text buttons.

5. Texture details (texture card and hover image)

  • Hover image: the second product image appears when a customer hovers over a product card. Turn this on with Product cards > Show texture image on hover. Use a close-up of the texture as the second image.
  • Texture card on the product page: by default it shows the text you type in the block. To show different text per product, create product metafields (for example custom.texture_title, custom.texture_note, custom.skin_types) and connect them to the block's settings with the dynamic source button. Skin types are written as a comma-separated list.
  • The card's "See texture" link jumps to the first product image whose alt text contains the word "texture".

6. Free samples in the cart

  1. Create sample products priced at 0 (for example "Glow Drops sample, 2 ml") and put them in a collection.
  2. Publish the sample products to the Online Store sales channel. Unlisted products don't appear in collections, so keep them active.
  3. In Theme settings > Cart, choose that collection as Samples collection and set Samples per order.
  4. Customers then pick samples in the cart drawer and on the cart page.

9. FAQ

Why do some image spots show drawings after install? Demo images are not transferred. Add your own images in the theme editor.

The routine bar is empty. Choose a collection for each step in the Routine bar section.

Swatches show as text buttons. The option isn't connected to the category color metafield yet (section 4).

The cart shows "Save 15%" but no discount applies. Offer text is only text. Create the matching automatic discount (section 3).

Samples don't appear in the cart. Check that the samples collection is set, the products are active and published to the Online Store, and that Samples per order is at least 1.

How do I translate the theme? Use Shopify's translation tools in Settings > Languages. All storefront text is in the theme's locale file.

Can I edit the code? Yes. Duplicate the theme first. Code changes aren't covered by support; consider hiring a Shopify Partner.
